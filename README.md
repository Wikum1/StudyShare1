# StudyShare

## Empowering Students Through Smarter Learning

StudyShare is a MERN-stack web application designed to enhance the academic experience of students by combining resource sharing and study planning into a single platform.

The platform enables students to upload, discover, and manage educational resources while organizing their academic schedules through an integrated study planner. By bringing collaboration and productivity together, StudyShare helps students stay focused, organized, and prepared for their academic goals.

---

## Features

### Resource Sharing

* Upload and share study materials.
* Browse resources uploaded by other students.
* Organize learning materials by subject.
* Quick access to academic content.

### Study Planner

* Create personalized study plans.
* Schedule tasks and study sessions.
* Weekly timetable view.
* Progress tracking for completed tasks.
* Calendar-based planning system.

### User Dashboard

* Centralized workspace for students.
* Manage resources and study plans.
* Monitor study progress and activity.

### Secure Authentication

* User registration and login.
* Protected routes and personalized content.

## Project Objectives

The primary objective of StudyShare is to provide students with a unified platform that:

* Encourages collaborative learning.
* Simplifies academic resource management.
* Improves study planning and time management.
* Enhances student productivity through organized workflows.

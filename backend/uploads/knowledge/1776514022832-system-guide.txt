StudyShare System Guide

Dashboard:
The dashboard is the main area where students can access all features. From the dashboard, students can upload resources, manage study plans, view notifications, and access their profile.

Upload Resource:
To upload a resource, go to the dashboard and click on the Upload Resource page. Fill in the required details such as title, description, and category. Then choose a file and submit it. After uploading, the resource will be available for other students.

My Resources:
Students can view all uploaded resources in the My Resources page. They can manage, view, and download their files from this section.

Study Planner:
Students can create study plans using the Study Planner page. They can add tasks, set deadlines, and organize their academic work efficiently.

Profile:
Students can view and update their personal details in the Profile section.

Forum / Wall:
Students can post questions or discussions on the Wall page. Other students can interact by commenting and reacting.

Notifications:
Students receive notifications when someone interacts with their posts or activities.

Search:
Students can search resources using keywords in the search bar. Special characters are not allowed in search input.
